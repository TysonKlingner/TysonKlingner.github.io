About

I am a fourth-year PhD student in mathematics at the University of Washington.

I am jointly advised by Sándor Kovács and Giovanni Inchiostro. My research interests are in algebraic geometry and birational geometry.

Research

My current research consists of understanding the moduli of higher dimensional varieties, and the birational structure of algebraic stacks.

You can find more information about my research on the research page.
